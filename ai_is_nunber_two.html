AIは大…もとい、話題じゃ。踏ん張って、もとい、振り絞ってみた 〜便秘予防の川柳と狂歌〜 _AI Is Number Two... I Mean, the Number One Topic!_

導入 / Introduction

よう皆の衆、元気でやっておるかのう。

Hey, folks! How have you been?

今日の与太話は、下の話の続きじゃ。

Today's silly story picks up where our little "below the belt" talk left off.

前編はこちらじゃ → AIで作ったトイレの落書を貼レルヤ否や?(小水編)

_Part 1 (the "number one" edition) is here → Poetic Restrooms & Sacred Warranties: From Japanese Toilet Verse to Divine Disclaimers

実はワシは今朝、庭いじりをしておったら、いきなりお腹のあたりがむずむずしてきてのう。さきほど、用を足してきたところじゃ。

So, this morning, while I was gardening, my belly suddenly started to feel restless. I just got back from the bathroom.

そういえば、働いておった頃は、出勤前にそういうことが多かったのじゃが、退職してからはリズムが変わってきたようでの。ちょいと調べてみたら、やはり生活習慣というものは、思いのほか腸に影響するらしいのう。

Back when I was working, this often happened before I left for the office. Since I retired, my rhythm has changed. I did a little reading, and it seems daily habits affect the gut more than I expected.

幸いワシは、それなりのリズムをつかめておるが、世の中には悩んでおられる方も多いと聞く。そこで、そんな方々のお役に立つかもしれんと、ネタを踏ん張って、もとい、振り絞ってみたのじゃ。

Luckily, I've found a fairly steady rhythm. But I hear plenty of people are struggling. So, hoping to help, I strained... I mean, I racked my brain for a few verses.

◆ 川柳の部 / Senryu (short poems)

1. レンチンの 待ち時間こそ スクワット
Microwave running—
those few minutes of waiting
are perfect for squats.

2. ロボ任せ 楽の代わりに 腸詰まる
Let the robot do it—
the price of the easy life:
a backed-up gut.

📝 In this image, the red cross-out is a pun: 超 (chō, "super") is corrected to 腸 (chō, "gut"). Same sound, different meaning. "Super clogged... I mean, gut clogged."

3. 草むしり 抜けば腹から 抜けていく
Pulling up weeds—
the garden gets lighter,
and so do you.

4. 物干しの 背伸びしゃがみが 腸の友
Reach up, bend down,
hanging the laundry:
your gut's best friend.

◆ 狂歌の部 / Kyoka (comic tanka)

5. 草を引き 洗濯干して 立ち座り 動く暮らしに 腸も整う
Pulling weeds, hanging laundry,
standing up and sitting down.
In a life that keeps moving,
the gut finds its rhythm too.

6. 楽させて くれる家電の ありがたさ 動かぬ腸に 保証切れかな
How grateful we are
for gadgets that make life easy—
but for a gut that won't move,
the warranty's expired.

7. ロボ任せ レンチン頼み 楽すれば 腸もお休み 脚下雲固ぞ
Robots clean, microwaves cook;
take it too easy, and your gut takes a break too.
Mind your feet—
kyakka unko.

📝 Note on #7
Kyakka unko (脚下雲固) is a pun on the Zen phrase kyakka shōko (脚下照顧 :
look at your own feet") . Unko is Japanese for poop. The pun doesn't translate, so I'm leaving it in Japanese. 😄

◆ ワシからの一言 / A Word from Me

便利な暮らしは、ありがたいものじゃ。ただ、楽をした分だけ体を動かさんようになると、腸もつられてお休みしてしまうのかもしれん。とはいえ、これは「かもしれん」という、ワシの見立てにすぎん。家電が便秘の原因じゃと、はっきり言えるわけではないぞい。

Modern life is wonderful. But if we move less because things are easier, maybe our gut also takes a rest. Of course, this is only my guess. I can't say that appliances cause constipation.

要は、草をむしるも、洗濯物を干すも、家の中でちょいと立ったりしゃがんだりするだけで、よいのじゃ。男も女も、誰でもな。

The point is simple: pull some weeds, hang some laundry, or just stand up and squat down a few times around the house. Anyone can do it, whoever you are.

ただし、便秘が長く続いたり、腹痛や血便があったりするときは、歌を詠んどる場合ではないぞい。まず病院へ行くのじゃ。ワシは医者ではないからのう。

But if constipation lasts a long time, or you have stomach pain or blood in your stool, this is no time for poems. Please see a doctor first. I am not one.

腸の保証は、一度切れると、腸器、もとい、長期延長保証が効かんからのう。

Once your gut's warranty runs out, even a long-term extended warranty won't help.

📝 Note
In Japanese, 腸器 (chōki, a made-up word meaning "gut organ") sounds exactly like 長期 (chōki, "long-term"). That's the pun!

ちなみに、最近のSNSでは、外国語翻訳が流行っておるが、ここまで気の利いた翻訳をしてくれる時代は、まだ当分さきじゃろうのう……🍵

免責 / Disclaimer

この助言の制作において実際の医師からのお言葉は用いてはおらん。困ったら、本物の先生に相談してくれ。

"No actual doctors were replaced in the making of this advice. Please consult a real one."

余談 / By the Way

ちなみに、最近は外国語翻訳が流行っておるが、洒落まで訳せる時代は、まだ先のようじゃのう。この記事の英語は、AIの助けを借りておるが、洒落の注釈は、ワシも一緒に頭をひねったのじゃ……🍵

By the way, translation tools are popular these days, but puns still seem out of their reach. An AI helped with the English here, but I scratched my head over the pun notes too. 🍵

締め / Ending

おっと、一杯🍶/🍺/🍷やる前に、ワシはまた庭🏡に戻るとするかの。
皆さんも、ちょいと立ち座り🚶/🪑などいかがじゃ?

Oops, before I start drinking 🍶/🍺/🍷, I think I'll go back to my garden 🏡.
Why not stand up and sit down🚶/🪑a few times yourself?

参考文献 / References & Notes for Curious Minds:

  1. On squatting and defecation
    Sikirov D. "Comparison of Straining During Defecation in Three Positions: Results and Implications for Human Health." Digestive Diseases and Sciences, 2003; 48(7):1201–1205. PMID: 12870773.
    Note: A small study of 28 healthy volunteers found that squatting reduced both the time and the perceived straining of defecation compared with sitting. It looked at toilet posture, not gardening, so the link to weeding is only a loose analogy.
  2. On constipation being more common in women
    Pinto Sanchez MI, Bercik P. "Epidemiology and Burden of Chronic Constipation." Canadian Journal of Gastroenterology, 2011; 25(Suppl B):11B–15B. (PMC3206560)
  3. On body clocks and the gut
    Voigt RM, Forsyth CB, Keshavarzian A. "Circadian Rhythms: A Regulator of Gastrointestinal Health and Dysfunction." Expert Review of Gastroenterology & Hepatology, 2019; 13(5):411–424. (PMC6533073)
    Note: A review noting that circadian rhythms regulate much of gut physiology, including motility. Some of the mechanisms are still described as speculative.
  4. 生活習慣と慢性便秘について / On lifestyle and chronic constipation
    日本消化管学会「便通異常症診療ガイドライン2023 慢性便秘症」(2023年7月刊行)
    案内ページ: https://jpn-ga.or.jp/blog/ct_for-members/8705/
    英語版: Ihara E, Manabe N, Ohkubo H, et al. "Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines for Chronic Constipation 2023." Digestion, 2025; 106(1):62–89. Published by The Japanese Gastroenterological Association.
    PMID: 39159626 / PMCID: PMC11825134 / DOI: 10.1159/000540912
    Note: The guidelines list lower physical activity as a risk factor and put lifestyle and diet improvement at the start of treatment. They do not study gardening specifically.
